# DS-001 --- SpectraLab Design Statement

**Document ID:** DS-001\
**Version:** 1.0\
**Status:** Approved\
**Applies from:** SpectraLab v0.7.0

> **This document defines the governing design principles of the
> SpectraLab project.**
>
> **Changes to this document shall be exceptional and require explicit
> project approval.**

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 1. Purpose

SpectraLab exists to provide a scientifically rigorous, transparent, and
reproducible environment for spectral measurements and analysis.

The project is founded on the principle that a measurement represents an
observation of reality. Once acquired, that observation shall remain
intact throughout its lifetime. Analysis methods may evolve, improve,
and expand, but the original measurement shall remain unchanged.

**Measure once. Save forever.**

# 2. Vision

The long-term vision of SpectraLab is to become a trusted scientific
platform for spectral measurements, optical analysis, and photographic
process evaluation.

Every result shall be reproducible, understandable, and traceable to its
source.

# 3. Design Philosophy

-   Scientific integrity before convenience.
-   Original measurements are immutable.
-   Analyses are reproducible and regenerated from original data.
-   Transparency is preferred over hidden automation.

# 4. Engineering Principles

-   Clear responsibilities.
-   Consistent architecture.
-   Stable public interfaces.
-   Automated testing.
-   Documentation is part of the implementation.

# 5. Scientific Principles

Recognised scientific standards shall be used whenever available.
Deviations shall be documented. Algorithms, interpolation methods and
numerical assumptions shall be explainable and scientifically motivated.

## 5.1 Open Software

SpectraLab is developed as open software because scientific software
benefits from openness.

The source code is available for inspection, allowing users, researchers
and developers to examine how results are produced, identify potential
errors, question assumptions and propose improvements.

No scientific software should assume it is free from mistakes.
Confidence is earned through transparency, reproducibility, peer review
and continuous improvement.

SpectraLab is not a commercial software product backed by contractual
support obligations or guaranteed service commitments. Users remain
responsible for evaluating whether the software is suitable for their
intended applications. The absence of commercial liability does not
diminish the project's commitment to scientific quality, which is
pursued through rigorous engineering, comprehensive testing, careful
documentation and open scientific review.

# 6. User Philosophy

SpectraLab helps users understand their measurements. Results should be
traceable, and the software should never hide assumptions or
uncertainties.

# 7. Long-Term Commitment

Development decisions favour long-term stability over short-term
convenience. Measurement archives should remain usable across future
versions whenever technically possible.

# 8. Closing Statement

SpectraLab is built upon scientific integrity, reproducibility,
transparency and respect for measured data.

Its openness reflects the belief that scientific software earns trust
not by claiming perfection, but by allowing its methods, assumptions and
implementation to be openly examined, discussed and continuously
improved.
