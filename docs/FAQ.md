# FAQ

## Why MATLAB first?

Because the target workflow is scientific, laboratory and educational MATLAB use.

## Why JSON?

Because saved spectral measurements should remain readable over time.

## Is v1.1.0 the current stable release?

Yes. v1.1.0 is the current stable release of SpectraLab.



It combines the established measurement and archive foundation with
registered scientific analyses, plotting and PDF reporting. Future releases
will continue to maintain compatibility with released archive formats
whenever possible.

## Where should I start?

Run:

```matlab
startup
measure_led
```

New users should read `README.md` and `docs/GETTING_STARTED.md`.
